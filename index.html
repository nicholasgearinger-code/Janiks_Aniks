Welcome to Janiks Aniks

A happy little world of mugs, gifts and everyday treasures.

Janiks Aniks grew from sharing colorful mugs and cute finds at our stall into a collection of little things worth smiling about. From character mugs and seasonal favorites to plushies, figurines and more, every display brings together something playful, practical or simply adorable.

These are real moments from our market displays—the tables of mugs, cozy plush friends and cheerful collections that have become part of our story. Now we're bringing that same joy of browsing to our online shop, too.
